giant steps

Coltrane's cadence in miniature: a plain ii–V–I is stretched through three key centres a major third apart, each reached by its own dominant. Press play to hear it, and watch the roots step down.

The skeleton is an ordinary ii–V–I: Dm7 … G7 → C. Coltrane fills the gap by tonicising two more keys on the way home.

The three resting points — A♭, E, C — are each a major third apart. Those leaps are the "giant steps." Every one is set up by its dominant 7th (E♭7→A♭, B7→E, G7→C), so the roots zig-zag m2 · P4 · m3 · P4 · m3 · P4 as they descend.
